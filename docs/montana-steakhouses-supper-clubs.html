Steaks and supper clubs

Visit Montana keeps its own listings of the state's steakhouses and supper clubs, from bars on the Hi-Line to Anaconda's Barclay II and Butte's last Meaderville-style supper club. We list the ones we could tie to one open place at the listing's address; many keep seasons, so call ahead.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Helena (3), Billings (3), Butte (2), Gallatin Gateway (2), Havre (2), Anaconda (1), Bearcreek (1), Great Falls (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if the state tourism office's own listing (Visit Montana) named it and an open listing at its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.

On your phone, Treasure State Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
