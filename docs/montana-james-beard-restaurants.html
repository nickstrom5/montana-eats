What counts as an honor here

The James Beard Foundation's awards, reported as facts rather than scores: Bozeman's Wild Crumb won Outstanding Bakery in 2026; Earl James Reynolds of Herb & Omni in Whitefish was a 2026 Best Chef: Mountain finalist; Helena's The Union was a 2025 Best New Restaurant finalist; and the 2025 and 2026 semifinalists. Where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place says so. Butte's Pekin Noodle Parlor, a 2023 America's Classic, closed in April 2026. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count.

James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation. Treasure State Eats is not affiliated with it.
