Terms of use

The plain-language terms for the Treasure State Eats app and this website. Last updated 2026-11-06.

What the app is

Treasure State Eats is a free guide to restaurants in Montana. It is provided as is, for personal use, without charge and without warranties of any kind.

Check before you go

Restaurants open, close, change their hours and change their menus, and seasonal places keep seasons. Dishes and founding years are what each place or a named source said when we checked in fall 2026. We work to keep the lists right, but we can't promise that any listing is current or complete. Call the restaurant before you make the trip.

Inspections and public records

The app and this site show no inspection results: Montana's counties post theirs on their own search pages, which each place links to, and nothing is published as data. We compute no grade. For official records, use each agency's own site.
