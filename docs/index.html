Built around how people here eat

Star ratings won't tell you which supper club the whole county drives to on a Saturday. Treasure State Eats is organized the way people here eat: steakhouses and supper clubs, and drive-ins, each place on Visit Montana's own listings and checked in fall 2026. Apple Maps' live card supplies ratings, hours and photos.

Free, with no catch

The app costs nothing, shows no ads and sells nothing inside, and there's no account to make. The lists ship inside the app, so they open right away and keep working when the signal drops out on the prairie.

What stays on your phone

Treasure State Eats collects nothing about you: no sign-in, no analytics, no ads. Location, if you turn it on, only puts the nearest places first, and your saved places are kept on the phone or iPad. The website has no cookies and no trackers. The privacy policy has the details.

Questions

Is Treasure State Eats free?

Completely: no price, no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create. It's an iPhone and iPad app on its way to the App Store, with no Android version yet; until it's out, the web search here works in any browser.

Where do the lists come from?

Two kinds of source. The steakhouse, supper club and drive-in lists start from Visit Montana's own listings, the state tourism office's, each place matched to an open listing at its address in fall 2026. The wider directory of 2,784 restaurants starts from open map listings (Overture Maps), filtered to the kinds that proved reliable where lists exist: in Helena, high-confidence listings matched a retail food permit more than eight times in ten.

Where can I get a good steak?

Our steakhouse and supper club list has 40 places on Visit Montana's own listings, from prairie-town bars to the cities and the mountains. In the web search, the list puts the nearest first once you share your location.

How do I check a Montana restaurant's inspection?

County and city sanitarians inspect restaurants. Flathead, Missoula, Yellowstone, Gallatin and Lewis and Clark counties post inspections on their own search pages, which their places link to; elsewhere the county health department keeps the reports. None are published as data, and we compute no grade.

Does it show which places serve alcohol?

No. The Department of Revenue licenses bars and restaurants, but its licensee list isn't published as data, so the app doesn't say either way.

Does it show ratings and reviews?

Not from us. Tapping a place opens Apple Maps' own card inside the app, with Apple's current ratings, hours, photos and directions. None of our lists is sorted by anyone's rating.

Does it need my location?

No. Location only matters for putting the nearest places first. If you share it, it stays on your iPhone or iPad and never reaches us, and the app works fine without it. On this website, Near me reads your location inside your browser and nowhere else.

A place has closed, or something's wrong. How do I tell you?

Write to nick@eatsranked.com with the place's name, its town and what changed. Corrections ship with the next data update.
